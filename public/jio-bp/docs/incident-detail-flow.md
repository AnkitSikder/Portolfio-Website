# Incident Detail Flow

The Incident Detail view provides a comprehensive, centralized record for investigating and tracking the lifecycle of an individual incident.

## Key Screens
*   **06-ro-incident-detail.png**: The comprehensive modal view for a specific incident (e.g., PR100007), showcasing metadata, timeline, and associated actions.

## Core Interactions
*   **Status & Severity Indicators**: Prominently displayed badges (e.g., "In Progress", "High Severity") for immediate context.
*   **Timeline / Event Log**: A vertical timeline visualizing the chronological history of the incident, from initial reporting through investigation steps to resolution.
*   **Action Items**: A dedicated section for assigning, tracking, and updating corrective and preventative actions (CAPA).
*   **Attachment Gallery**: A grid view of uploaded evidence (photos, documents) with lightbox viewing capabilities.
*   **Collaborative Investigation**: Features for adding comments, requesting more information from the reporter, and cross-functional communication.

## UI/UX Notes
*   Employs a two-column layout within the modal: primary details and timeline on the left, metadata and action items on the right.
*   High information density balanced by clear typographic hierarchy and whitespace.
*   Uses a persistent header within the modal for quick access to core actions (e.g., Export, Print, Close).
