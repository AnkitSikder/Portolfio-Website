# HO Dashboard

The Head Office (HO) Dashboard in the Jio-bp Incident Management System is the apex level of the system, designed for national-level executive oversight and policy deployment.

## Key Screens
*   **01-ho-dashboard-full.png**: The full HO dashboard view, showing nationwide aggregations and high-level strategic KPI cards.

## Core Interactions
*   **Strategic KPI Cards**: The Key Performance Indicators on the HO dashboard represent national-level figures and are critical for executive reporting and broad systemic risk analysis.
*   **Executive Incident Feed**: A streamlined view of high-severity or highly-escalated incidents that require executive attention.
*   **Policy & Compliance Dissemination**: Allows Head Office to inject policies and directives that trickle down into the "Learning from Incidents" modules of ASRs and ROs across the country.
*   **Macro Filtering**: Global search and filters operate at the broadest scale, enabling the tracking of trends by state, zone, or facility type.

## UI/UX Notes
*   Consistent aesthetic with RO and ASR dashboards, providing a unified experience for any stakeholder navigating the system.
*   Emphasizes clean typography and uncluttered data visualizations to make executive-level decision making as efficient as possible.
*   The context clearly indicates a Head Office persona, adjusting available functionalities accordingly.
